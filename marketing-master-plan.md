## 3. Ads Kon Platform? — Verdict

### 🥇 #1 Meta Ads (Facebook + Instagram) — **$3–5/day**
- **Keno:** Apnar product (fashion, beauty, gadgets) 100% visual. Bangladesh theke manage kora sohoj. CPC $0.05–0.15 — sobcheye sosta. Retargeting (je cart felche take abar dekhabe) best-in-class.
- **Campaign:** 1 Sales campaign → 3 ad sets (US/UK/AU · KSA/UAE · CA/EU), 1 ad set $1–1.5/day.

### 🥈 #2 TikTok Ads — $5/day (Tokhon jokhon 3–5 ta Reels viral hobe)
- Gen-Z gadgets + beauty er jonno best, kintu video content banaite hobe.

### ❌ #3 Google Search Ads — **NA**
- "women sandals" CPC $0.80–1.50 — apnar $23 er product e profit nite parbe na. **Google Search e taka dite hobe na.**

### ✅ #4 Google Merchant Center — **FREE, age eta koren**
- Free product listings on Google Shopping. Taka lagbe na. Shop name, feed submit (Shopify → Google & YouTube app), 1 din e live.

> ⚠️ **Sotto sotti:** Ads start korar age **payment test ✅ + CJ photo fix + price fix** lagbe. 30 checkout → 0 order hole prottek click er taka nosto hobe. **Ads = petrol, store = gari. Gari jodi engine kharaap, petrol dileo jabe na.**
